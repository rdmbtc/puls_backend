# Will Circle (USDC) complete its IPO in 2026?

**Thesis: YES — probability 67%**

Circle filed for NASDAQ listing (ticker: CRCL) in early 2025 after its SPAC attempt collapsed in 2022. The company is profitable, USDC supply has recovered to $35B+, and the regulatory environment is the most crypto-friendly in years. Stablecoin legislation (Clarity for Payment Stablecoins Act) passed the House — this de-risks Circle's core business model for public market investors.

**Arguments for YES:**
- Circle's revenue model is clean: interest income on USDC reserves ($35B+ at 5% = ~$1.75B annually)
- Profitable and growing — rare for crypto companies seeking IPO
- Regulatory tailwind: stablecoin legislation gives USDC legal clarity
- Market appetite for crypto exposure is strong (Coinbase stock 3x from 2023 lows)
- Previous SPAC failure means Circle has been "IPO-ready" for years — the S-1 is polished
- Strategic timing: list before the cycle peaks to capture maximum valuation

**Arguments for NO:**
- Market conditions: if equities correct, IPO windows close fast
- Competition from PayPal's PYUSD and bank-issued stablecoins could pressure USDC market share
- Regulatory risk: if stablecoin legislation stalls or passes with unfavorable terms
- Private market valuation expectations ($9B+) may not clear public market scrutiny
- Tether dominance (65% market share) is the elephant in the room

**Key catalysts:** SEC S-1 effectiveness, stablecoin bill Senate vote, Q2 2026 earnings (to show profitability trend).

**Play:** YES at $0.60-0.65 offers good risk/reward. Circle has the strongest fundamentals of any crypto company seeking public listing. The main risk is macro (IPO window closing), not company-specific.
