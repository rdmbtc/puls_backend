# Will NVIDIA stock hit $200 by end of 2026?

**Thesis: YES — probability 55%**

NVIDIA (NVDA) trades around $135 mid-June 2026 after a 10:1 stock split. The AI infrastructure buildout is still accelerating: hyperscaler capex guidance for 2026 totals $250B+, with NVIDIA capturing 70-80% of GPU spend. Blackwell B200 shipments are ramping, and demand exceeds supply through Q3. $200 implies a ~$5T market cap — aggressive but not insane given the revenue trajectory.

**Arguments for YES:**
- Data center revenue growing 80%+ YoY — Blackwell cycle hasn't peaked yet
- AI inference demand is the next leg: enterprises deploying LLMs need GPUs at scale
- Software/HPC moat deepening: CUDA ecosystem lock-in is real, competitors (AMD, Intel) aren't closing the gap
- Sovereign AI: governments building national AI infrastructure = new demand vertical
- Forward P/E of ~35x is reasonable for a company growing earnings 50%+ annually

**Arguments for NO:**
- $200 requires a 48% move — large-cap stocks rarely sustain that pace
- Customer concentration risk: top 5 hyperscalers are 60%+ of revenue — any capex cut hurts
- Custom silicon threat: Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Microsoft Maia could erode GPU share
- Export controls to China remain a headwind (~10% revenue at risk)
- If AI capex disappoints (ROI skepticism grows), the multiple compresses fast

**Key catalysts:** Q2 earnings (Aug), hyperscaler capex guidance updates, any AI ROI backlash.

**Play:** YES at $0.50 is a momentum bet on the AI cycle continuing. The risk is a capex air pocket — if any hyperscaler signals reduced GPU spend, this drops to $0.35 overnight.
