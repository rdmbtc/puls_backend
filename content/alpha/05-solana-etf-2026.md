# Will a spot Solana ETF be approved in 2026?

**Thesis: YES — probability 58%**

The SEC approved spot Bitcoin ETFs in January 2024 and Ethereum ETFs in May 2024. The logical next candidates are Solana and XRP. Several issuers (VanEck, Grayscale, 21Shares) have filed S-1s for spot SOL ETFs. The political environment is crypto-friendly — the current SEC chair has signaled openness to "commodity" classification for proof-of-stake tokens.

**Arguments for YES:**
- SEC has approved BTC and ETH ETFs — the precedent is set
- Solana is the clear #3 by market cap and institutional interest is surging (SOL futures on CME)
- Grayscale's SOL trust trading at 2-3% premium signals real demand
- Political pressure: crypto PAC money influenced 2024 elections — anti-crypto stance is politically costly
- SEC staff has been engaging with issuers on S-1 amendments (not rejecting outright)

**Arguments for NO:**
- SOL is a proof-of-stake token — SEC may classify it as a security (staking = investment contract)
- No SOL futures ETF exists yet (usually a prerequisite step)
- Market manipulation concerns: SOL has had network outages and validator concentration
- SEC could delay indefinitely — the "engage but never approve" playbook
- Legal challenges: if any court rules SOL is a security, the ETF is dead

**Key catalysts:** SEC decision deadlines (Grayscale filing: Aug 2026), any SOL security classification ruling.

**Play:** YES at $0.50 is a coin flip with asymmetric upside. If the SEC signals engagement (positive S-1 comments), this moves to $0.70+ fast. Size small, manage risk.
